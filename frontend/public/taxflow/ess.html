UAE Document Requirements
Visa / Entry Permit

UAE Labour Law requires valid entry permit. HR alerts at 90-day and 30-day pre-expiry.

Emirates ID (EID)

Federal Identity Authority mandatory. Alerts at 60-day and 30-day milestones.

Passport

Most UAE contracts require 6+ months validity. Alert when less than 6 months remain.

Work Permit / Labour Card

MOL Work Permit must be renewed before expiry. Linked to employment contract validity.
