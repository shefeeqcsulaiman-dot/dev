FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Is ETaxFlow officially FTA-registered?
+

Yes. ETaxFlow's VAT module generates FTA-compliant VAT201 returns and supports TRN validation, input VAT recovery, and zero-rated export transactions in line with UAE VAT Law Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017.

Which UAE banks are supported?
+

Emirates NBD, FAB (First Abu Dhabi Bank), ADCB, Mashreq, DIB (Dubai Islamic Bank), HSBC UAE, RAK Bank, CBD, and Standard Chartered UAE are fully supported for bank reconciliation and payment processing.

Can I migrate from QuickBooks or Tally?
+

Yes. Our import tool handles QuickBooks QBO exports, Tally XML, and Excel-based chart of accounts. Opening balances and historical data are imported in minutes with guided mapping.

How does AI invoice extraction work?
+

Upload any PDF, image, or scanned invoice. Our AI reads supplier names, TRN numbers, line items, VAT amounts, and dates — then posts to the correct accounts automatically. Accuracy exceeds 98% on UAE-format invoices.

Is my data stored in the UAE?
+

Yes. All data is stored on UAE-region cloud infrastructure with 256-bit AES encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit, fully compliant with UAE Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).
